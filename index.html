Questions, answered

What is The Baker's Directory?

The Baker's Directory is a free directory for finding trusted bakeries and baking suppliers in the Philippines. Whether you're after the best sourdough near you, a home baker who delivers in Metro Manila, or a flour and packaging supplier for your own bakery, we're building one place to find them.

Is The Baker's Directory free to use?

Yes. Searching and browsing the directory is free, and always will be. Bakeries and suppliers can also claim a free listing.

What areas does it cover?

We're starting in the Philippines — Metro Manila first, then nationwide. Support for Filipino bakeries that ship internationally, and for the global Filipino community abroad, is on the roadmap.

Can bakeries and suppliers list their business?

Absolutely. Home-based and storefront bakeries can be listed, alongside flour, ingredient, equipment, and packaging suppliers. Sign up above to be among the first featured at launch.

Can I send Filipino baked goods to family in the Philippines from abroad?

It's coming. We're building a way for overseas Filipinos to send artisan bread and baked goods to loved ones back home. Join the list to hear first.
